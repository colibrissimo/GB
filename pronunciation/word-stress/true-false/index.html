True or false?

Word stress, part 1. Rules and words, as fast as you can.

How do you want to play?

  • Two kinds of question: a rule (is it true?) and a word with its stressed syllable in capitals (is the stress in the right place?).
  • Click TRUE or FALSE, or press T or F.
  • The clock stops while you read the answer. Press Enter or Next to go on.
  • Say each word out loud before you answer.
  • Sit side by side. Left player: S = TRUE, D = FALSE. Right player: K = TRUE, L = FALSE.
  • The first one to answer takes the question. Right: +1 for you. Wrong: +1 for the other player. So don't guess.
  • The keys wake up a moment after the question appears: read it first.
  • The highest score after 16 questions wins.
