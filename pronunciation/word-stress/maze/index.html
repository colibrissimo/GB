Stress maze

Word stress, part 1. Find your way from START to the flag, stepping only on the words that follow the rule.

How do you want to play?

  • Three rounds. Each round has one rule: the stress is on the 1st, the 2nd or the 3rd syllable.
  • Move one square at a time: up, down, left or right. Only one way through is right.
  • Say each word out loud before you step on it. Your ears know more than you think.
  • A wrong word turns into a wall and adds 5 seconds to your time.
  • Three rounds. Each round has one rule: the stress is on the 1st, the 2nd or the 3rd syllable.
  • Each player has their own maze, with different words. Take turns: one step each, up, down, left or right.
  • Right word: +10. Wrong word: −5, and it turns into a wall.
  • First to the flag: +20. The other player still gets their last turn, so you both play the same number of turns.
  • The highest score after three rounds wins, not the fastest.
