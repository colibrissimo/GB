Short or long?

A listening warm-up. You will hear a word. You will not see it until every team has answered.

  1. Each team needs a sheet of paper and a pen.
  2. Listen. Agree as a team. Write your answer big and hide it.
  3. When the teacher says "cards up", every team shows at the same time. No changing after that.
  4. Round 1: short /ɪ/ or long /iː/? Round 2: which of the two words did you hear?
