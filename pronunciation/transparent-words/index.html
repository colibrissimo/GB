Transparent words

Food culture, coffee culture. Session 1.

The rule

A transparent word is the same word in French and in English. Same spelling, or nearly, and the same meaning: culture, tradition, festival, salad, sauce, dessert, chocolate.

They are the easiest words to read and the hardest to say, because you already believe you know them.

2 · The coffee menu

Five words, five different traps. Tap any of them to hear it.

espressoThere is no x. Not expresso, whatever the menu says at home. es-PRESS-oh
latteThe accent is at the front, and the ending is ay. LAH-tay
cappuccinoThe cc is a ch sound, and the u is oo. cap-oo-CHEE-noh
americanoFive syllables, one of them said. uh-mer-i-CAH-noh
mochaThe ch is a k. MOH-kuh
All five of these are Italian. French borrowed them and so did English, and the two languages did it differently. Nobody is saying them wrong in their own language. You are learning a second set of habits for the same five words.

3 · Un café, deux cafés

This is the grammar point and the pronunciation point at the same time, on the same word.

coffee, uncountable

The substance. Some coffee. A lot of coffee. How much coffee?

Coffee is made from the roasted seeds of the Coffea plant.

a coffee, countable

The cup. A coffee. Two coffees. How many coffees?

How many coffees have you had today?

So the plural exists, and you will need it on your first morning abroad:

onees-PRESS-oh
twoes-PRESS-ohz, with a z sound, because the word ends in a vowel

The same split runs through the whole worksheet: fruit and fruits, cheese and cheeses, tea and teas. One is the stuff, the other is the kinds or the servings.

4 · The -ture family

One ending, one rule, and it will follow you all semester. Tap to hear.

-ture is said chuh
and it is never the syllable you lean on

culture texture mixture temperature agriculture

cultureKUL-chuh. No r sound at the end.
textureTEKS-chuh. You will need this one to describe food.
mixtureMIKS-chuh.
temperatureTEM-pruh-chuh. Four syllables written, three said: the -pe- vanishes.
agricultureAG-ri-kul-chuh. The accent is on the very first syllable.

The family is bigger than this: nature, future, manufacture, structure, moisture. Same ending, same rule, every time.

5 · Four that break on a sound

The rest of this lesson is about where the accent falls. These four go wrong on a sound instead, or on a syllable that is simply not there.

chocolateThree syllables in French, two in English. The middle o is not said at all. CHOK-lut
vegetableFour syllables written, three said. VEJ-tuh-bul
sugarThe s is a sh. SHOOG-uh session 3
dessertTwo s letters, one z sound, accent at the back. di-ZURT
Careful with dessert and desert. One s is DEZ-ut, which is sand. Two is di-ZURT, which is pudding. The spelling is the only warning you get.

The syllable that disappears in chocolate, vegetable and temperature has a name, and it is the whole of next session.
