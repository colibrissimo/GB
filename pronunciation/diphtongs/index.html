Diphthongs

The brewing process. Session 4. Two vowel sounds inside one syllable.

The rule

2 vowels  /  1 single syllable
Three words from the brewhouse. Three diphthongs.
grain · pint · roast
Why this is hard for a French person: French vowels hold still. They start somewhere and stay there. English keeps moving. Say grain in French, then in English: the French one is one sound, the English one starts on e and slides up to i.

1 · The mirror test

let  →  late

Watch your lower jaw. On let it stays where it is. On late it should move upward, a small amount, while you are still saying the vowel.

let late  ·  pen pain  ·  test taste  ·  get gate

hop  →  hope

On hope the jaw moves forward, and the lips round as it goes. Yes, hop: the plant is the short one. Say it French and it is right.

hop hope  ·  not note  ·  cost coast  ·  rod road

2 · The eight sounds

The right-hand column is the useful part: most of the time you can tell from the spelling alone.

the Agrain ale taste flavour stage carbonate fermentation · ai, ay, ei, -ation, and a + consonant + silent e
the Ipint rye dry pipe mild light · igh, y at the end, and i + consonant + silent e
the Oroast toasty foam cold oats aroma · oa, and o + consonant + silent e
OYboil boiling oil spoil choice · oi, oy
OWstout lautering brown sour cloudy amount · ou, ow, au in lautering
AIRair airlock share prepare compare · air, and a + r + silent e
EARbeer clear year cereal near · ee, ea + r
SUREmature sure pure tour secure · u + r + silent e, our
Two glides in three letters. IPA is said letter by letter: the I, then P, then the A. Eye-pee-ay. Only the P holds still.
The false friend. process is the O in British English: PROH-cess. Not the French processus. And hops is not: short o, jaw still.

3 · The maze

Your own vocabulary, laid out in a grid. Start on GRAIN, get to BEER. One step at a time, up, down, left or right, never diagonally, and you may only stand on a word that glides.

 

4 · Say it

In each pair, both words have the same glide. Tap to hear.

grain = pale
boil = spoil
roast = foam
pint = rye
beer = clear
air = share
sure = mature
stout = sour

Now out loud, slowly, watching the jaw move. The underlined vowels glide.
