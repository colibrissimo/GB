The sound TH

The letters <th> are two sounds, and neither of them exists in French.

The rule

The mouth position: tip of the tongue between the teeth, and blow gently. Not behind the teeth. Between them. You should be able to see it in a mirror.

the quiet th

No vibration. Hand on the throat: nothing.

think, three, threat, thousand

method, ethics, healthy

growth, health, truth, both

the buzzing th

Vibration. Hand on the throat: a buzz.

the, they, this, that, there

other, whether, together

breathe, bathe, with

Content word → quiet   ·   Grammar word → buzzing
think, three, threat are things. the, this, that, they are glue.

Two more rules, for the middle and the end of a word:

① In the middle: buzzing between two vowels (other, whether, together). But a Greek or Latin word stays quiet anyway: method, pathogen, synthetic, and ethics, which sits between two vowels and stays quiet regardless, because the word is Greek.

② At the end: the noun is quiet, the verb buzzes. breath (noun, quiet) but breathe (verb, buzzing). Same for bath and bathe. Growth, health, truth are nouns, so they are quiet.

The exceptions, where <th> really is a plain t, are: Thailand, Thames, Thomas, Anthony, Theresa, apartheid.

1 · The throat test

  1. Put the flat of your hand on the front of your throat.
  2. Say think. Hold the first sound: thhhhh. Your hand feels nothing. That is the quiet th.
  3. Now say this. Hold it: thhhhh. Your hand feels a buzz. That is the buzzing th.
  4. Go back and forth: think, this, think, this. The tongue does not move. Only the buzz switches on and off.
Same tongue. Same teeth. Same air.
The only difference is whether your throat is switched on.

2 · Zat is ze question

French does not have these sounds, so we borrow the nearest one. The trouble is that the nearest one is a different word. Tap either word to hear it.

Notice that there is no single wrong sound. Depending on the word we reach for s, f, z, d or t. That is why "just say z" does not fix it, and why the tongue has to go between the teeth instead. threat and treat are the pair to remember: a GM crop can be one or the other, and the only difference is your tongue.

4 · The GMO words

Words you will actually say in this debate. Tap to hear, then say each one with the tongue between the teeth.

health is the one to get right. Two sounds French does not have, one at each end: the h at the front and the quiet th at the back, tongue between the teeth. Not "elt", not "elf". And ethics is not "etics": the French word has a t in the mouth, the English one does not.
