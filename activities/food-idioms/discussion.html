Food idioms · Talk first

In pairs. Two minutes a question, then swap who starts.

1What is your favourite fruit or vegetable, and why do you like it?
2Have you ever used a food idiom in French? Give an example, and try to translate it.
3How often do you try new fruits or vegetables? What decides it?
4In what ways does food bring people together?
5Do you know something surprising about the history of a fruit or vegetable?

The button chooses a question at random, so nobody argues about which one.
