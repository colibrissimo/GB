Bioremediation Memory

Cleaning up pollution with living things. Find each English word and its French partner.

All the cards start face down. Turn over two cards.

If one is the English word and the other is its French translation, it's a pair: the cards stay open. If not, look at them carefully, then click to turn them back.

Remember where everything is, and find all the pairs in as few moves as you can.

Game

Twist: when your two cards are not a pair, they turn back over and swap places: each card moves to where the other one was. Your memory has to move with them!
Van Chung invented this twist at the age of 12. Computer scientist Ken Perlin turned it into a game.

Bonus: when your two cards are not a pair, they get the numbers 1 and 2. Then you choose a face-down card, and the three cards move round: 1 goes to the place of 2, 2 goes to the place of your card, and your card goes to the place of 1. Plan your moves!
Ken Perlin's own version of Van Chung's twist.
